\PosterHeading[PosterHardness]{03}{Why pathwidth is hard}

\textbf{Source problem.} In \emph{Chosen Maximum Outdegree}, orient
weighted edges so that each vertex respects its outgoing-weight bound.
\PosterPara
\textbf{Choose.} Player 1 encodes an orientation by choosing a side of
each edge gadget.
\PosterPara
\textbf{Challenge.} Player 2 tests whether a vertex exceeds its bound.
A feasible orientation exists exactly when Player 1 can force a win.
\PosterSpace{6}

\input{figures/hardness}
\PosterSpace{6}

The construction bounds the new pathwidth by a function of the source
width. A constant-size gadget then transfers the result to undirected graphs.
\PosterPara
{\PosterSmallFont\color{PosterMuted}
  \textbf{Pathwidth:} overlapping vertex bags arranged in a path;
  width $=$ largest bag size minus one.
  \par}
