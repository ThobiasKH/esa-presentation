\PosterHeading{04}{Why tree partitions help}

Partition the vertices into bags arranged in a tree. Edges stay inside
a bag or join adjacent bags. Each bag has at most $k$ vertices.
\PosterPara
On simple graphs, a parent--child cut has at most $k^2$ edges:
the \textbf{ports} through which play crosses the boundary.
\PosterSpace{6}

\input{figures/algorithm}
\PosterSpace{6}

\textbf{Summarize.} An interface type records entry and return behavior,
the player to move, and the remaining type after an excursion.
\PosterPara
\textbf{Count.} Children of the same type are interchangeable.
Store their multiplicities and process the bags bottom-up.
\PosterPara
{\PosterSmallFont\color{PosterMuted}
  For fixed $k$, the type universe is finite. The compressed local games
  yield a total running time of $n^{f(k)}$.
  \par}
